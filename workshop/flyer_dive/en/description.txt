[h1]Flying Charge Overhaul[/h1]
Flyers now charge like flyers. Dragons, griffons, pegasi, manticores and every other flying unit dive in at an angle, land carrying their speed, and plough into the enemy line until the bodies they hit slow them down.

[h2]What changes[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Dive and plough.[/b] The charge comes in at a shallow angle instead of dropping straight down. On landing the flyer keeps moving through the enemy rank; only collisions slow it, and those collisions deal the engine's own impact damage.
[*][b]Braced lines hold.[/b] Units with high charge resistance are much less likely to be ploughed through: a flyer that dives into the front of a braced unit is slowed by the collision itself. Ground charges are unchanged. Can be adjusted with MCT.
[*][b]More Charge Reflection damage.[/b] Charge Reflection deals significantly more damage to diving flyers. Can be adjusted with MCT.
[*][b]Whole units land together.[/b] Multi-entity flyers no longer bob back up into the air when the leader touches down.
[*][b]Fairer take-off.[/b] Take-off is judged by body size, per model, and by the time since the last melee hit. Models that have taken off fly on with their unit's orders.
[*][b]Breath in melee.[/b] Dragon breath and similar breath abilities can be used while engaged or grounded. Can be adjusted with MCT.
[*][b]Tuned flyers.[/b] Per-flyer charge speeds set by role and lore; every flyer gets a moving attack; large flyers are 15% lighter and very large flyers 25% lighter (flying war machines and chariots unchanged).
[/list]
Applies to both sides, AI included.

[h2]Requirements[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]memreader Plus (win64 / Proton)[/b] is required for the dive, braced-line and take-off rules. Without it those parts switch off and only the animation and stat changes apply.
[/list]

[h2]Multiplayer[/h2]
Multiplayer campaign/battle supported.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*]Any mod that changes the same flyer unit rows, mounts or battle entities wins or loses by load order.
[*]This mod replaces the vanilla table that stops abilities in melee. A mod that ships the same table conflicts.
[/list]

[h2]Notes[/h2]
Each battle writes three small log files to the game folder (flyer_dive.txt, flyer_takeoff.txt, flyer_breath.txt). Include them when you report a problem.
