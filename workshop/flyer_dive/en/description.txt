[h1]Flyer Dive & Take-off[/h1]

Flying units charge like flying units: a dive keeps its speed when it lands, and a flyer caught in melee can take off again.

[h2]What it does[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Physics dive.[/b] A flying unit that dives onto the ground keeps its speed when it lands. Only the bodies it actually hits slow it down, so a dive carries into the enemy line instead of stopping dead at touchdown.
[*][b]Take-off from melee.[/b] A flyer engaged in melee can take off again. Each flying model is judged on its own: a model hit by a melee attack in the last few seconds cannot take off, the others can. The smaller the body, the longer it stays down: small 4.5 s, medium 4.0 s, large 3.5 s, very large 3.0 s (a ridden unit goes by its mount). Contact alone, ranged hits and charge impacts do not hold it.
[*][b]Charge speed per flyer.[/b] Every flyer has its own charge speed, set by its role and background (light skirmishers fastest, dragons slower but heavier), plus longer charge distances and a collision attack on its first contact.
[*]Every flying unit, both sides, the AI included.
[/list]

[h2]Balance submod (optional)[/h2]
Breath attacks while engaged in melee and the lower mass of large flyers are no longer part of this mod. They are in the optional [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=SUBMOD_ID_PLACEHOLDER]Flyer Dive & Take-off: Balance[/url] submod, which needs this mod.

[h2]Requirements[/h2]
[list]
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3811098873]memreader Plus[/url] (required). Without it the dive and take-off rules stay off and those two behave as in the base game.
[/list]

[h2]Multiplayer[/h2]
[list]
[*]Single player: works.
[*]Multiplayer campaign battles: works when every player runs the same version (memreader Plus checks it).
[*]Multiplayer custom and quick battles: works (the game's own mod check keeps both sides on one version).
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Mods that overwrite the same flying unit rows win or lose by load order.
