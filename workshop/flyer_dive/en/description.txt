[h1]Flying Charge Overhaul[/h1]
Flyers now charge like flyers. Dragons, griffons, pegasi, manticores and every other flying unit dive in at an angle, land carrying their speed, and plough into the enemy line until the bodies they hit slow them down. Multi-entity flyers land together, and models that have taken off keep following orders instead of being pulled back down by models still held on the ground.

[h2]What changes[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Dive and plough.[/b] The charge comes in at a shallow angle instead of dropping straight down. Flyers now actually dive into the enemy and deal impact damage.
[*][b]Whole units land together.[/b] Every model of a multi-entity flyer lands with its unit; no single model drops alone at its starting spot and splits the unit in two.
[*][b]No bounce after landing.[/b] Multi-entity flyers no longer get pulled back up into the air when the leader touches down.
[*][b]Fairer take-off.[/b] Take-off is now judged per model, and by the time since the last melee hit.
[*][b]More obedient.[/b] Models that have taken off fly on with their unit's orders and are no longer pulled back down by models still held on the ground.
[*][b]Moving attack animation.[/b] Every flyer gets a moving attack.
[*][b]Breath in melee.[/b] Dragon breath and similar breath abilities can be used while engaged or grounded. Can be adjusted with MCT.
[*][b]MCT settings.[/b] With the Mod Configuration Tool you can switch breath in melee on or off, set how strongly braced units resist a dive, and set how much Charge Reflection damage flying units take.
[/list]
Applies to both sides, AI included.

[h2]Requirements[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]memreader Plus (win64 / Proton)[/b] is required for the dive, braced-line and take-off rules. Without it those parts switch off and only the animation and stat changes apply.
[/list]

[h2]Multiplayer[/h2]
Multiplayer campaign/battle supported.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*]Playing with SFO: Grimhammer III? Also subscribe to the [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3843730179]SFO compatibility submod[/url].
[*]Any mod that changes the same flyer unit rows, mounts or battle entities wins or loses by load order.
[*]This mod replaces the vanilla table that stops abilities in melee. A mod that ships the same table conflicts.
[/list]
