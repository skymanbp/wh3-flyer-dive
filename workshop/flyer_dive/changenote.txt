- Pack renamed to flyer_dive_takeoff.pack: you may have to tick the mod again in the launcher; delete a hand-copied ld_air_dive.pack.
- Fixed: riders coming apart from pegasus-type mounts in a dive.
- Take-off from melee is now judged per model, by body size.
- More Charge Reflection damage: halberd units are a greater threat to large flying units.
- New optional setting (Mod Configuration Tool): breath in melee can be turned off. Default on.
- This version does not pair with the previous one in multiplayer.

- 包名改为 flyer_dive_takeoff.pack：可能需要在启动器里重新勾选一次；手动拷进 data 的 ld_air_dive.pack 请删掉。
- 修复：天马类坐骑俯冲时骑手与坐骑分离。
- 脱离近战改为每个兵模单独计算，按体型。
- 增加冲锋反制造伤，增加长戟部队对飞行大型部队的威胁。
- 新增可选设置（Mod Configuration Tool）：可以关掉陷入近战时的吹息，默认开。
- 本版与上一版在多人游戏中互不配对。
