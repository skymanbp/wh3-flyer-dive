- Flying units no longer carry a collision attack. Their charge damage is the game's own impact, from mass and speed, so light flyers such as bats and Furies hit far less hard than before.
- Furies are back to the base game's charge speed.
- Flying units keep the base game's mass (the 15% / 25% cut is gone).
- A landed flyer keeps pushing until the bodies in front of it stop it, as base-game cavalry does.
- Braced units resist a dive by mass and their own charge resistance only. Heavy flyers now break through infantry with low charge resistance, such as swordsmen; braced spears and halberds hold mid-weight flyers; dragons can still break through.
- MCT: the defaults of 'Braced mass against a dive' and 'Dive slowing against braced units' are now 1. If you changed them before, set them back to 1.

- 飞行部队不再带冲撞攻击，冲锋伤害只有游戏自己按质量和速度算的冲击。蝙蝠、怒妖这类轻型飞行部队的冲锋伤害大幅降低。
- 怒妖的冲锋速度回到原版。
- 飞行部队保持原版质量（不再降 15% / 25%）。
- 飞行部队落地后会一直往前冲，直到被前面的身体挡停，与原版骑兵相同。
- 阵列齐整的部队抵御俯冲只看质量和自身的冲锋抗性：重型飞行部队能冲穿剑士这类冲锋抗性低的步兵，阵列齐整的长矛与长戟挡得住中等重量的飞行部队，巨龙依然能冲穿。
- MCT：「阵列齐整时抵御俯冲的质量倍数」与「俯冲撞上阵列齐整部队时的减速比例」的默认值改为 1。以前改过这两项的，请改回 1。
