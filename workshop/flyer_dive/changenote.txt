- Braced units with high charge resistance are much less likely to be ploughed through by a diving flyer. Ground charges are unchanged.
- Charge Reflection deals significantly more damage to diving flyers.
- Multi-model flying units: models that have taken off fly on with their unit's orders.
- New Mod Configuration Tool settings: braced lines against dives (on/off), their strength, and the Charge Reflection damage. In a multiplayer campaign the host's settings apply.
- This version does not pair with the previous one in multiplayer.
